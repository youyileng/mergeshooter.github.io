18. Account Deletion

You may permanently delete your account and the data associated with it directly from within the game.

  1. Open the game.
  2. Tap your profile icon to open Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the warning prompt.

What is deleted: local game save (progress, coins, achievements, tanks, skills, inventory), Google Play Games cloud save, in-app purchase entitlements, Firebase Analytics user profile, and locally stored preferences and tutorial flags.

What is retained: aggregated and anonymized analytics that cannot reasonably be linked back to you, and records we are legally required to keep (for example, financial records of completed purchases retained for tax purposes).

Timing: Local deletion is immediate. Cloud and backend deletions complete within 30 days. Once deletion is finalized it cannot be undone.

If you cannot access the in-app option (for example, because you uninstalled the game), email lengyy6@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the Google Play Games user ID or display name you used.
